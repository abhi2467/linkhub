About LinkHub

A curated collection of useful, fun, and amazing websites — all in one place.

What is LinkHub?

LinkHub is a handpicked library of the internet's best gems. From productivity tools and learning resources to quirky time-wasters and creative inspiration — we gather the sites that make the web wonderful.

Why LinkHub?
  • Discover amazing sites – no more digging through search results.
  • Everything in one dashboard – your favourite tools, games, articles, and more.
  • No account, no tracking – your privacy matters, and we respect it.
  • Designed for joy – a clean, calming space to explore and enjoy.
How to explore
Start on the Homepage

Get a quick overview of your collection, see your most important links, and jump back to recently visited sites.

Browse All Links

Search by name or tag, filter by category, or mark favourites. Hit the shuffle button to discover something new and unexpected.

Our philosophy

We believe the internet is full of hidden treasures. LinkHub is our attempt to surface the best of them — not with algorithms, but with a human touch. It’s a collection of wonder, built for curiosity and joy.
